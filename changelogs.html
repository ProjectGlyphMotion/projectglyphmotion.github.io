v4.5 Architectural Overhaul

feat(v4.5): 5-stage async Batch-8 FP16 tracking engine, 4K CUDA pipeline, 128 MB Drive streaming & changelog overhaul

v4.5 unlocks massive multi-core CPU, RTX Tensor Core, and I/O parallelism across every stage of GlyphMotion without altering core UnifiedMode, PID, or HFDR perceptual algorithms—boosting 1080p throughput from 22.90 to 90.92 FPS (3.97×) and 3440×1440 throughput from 18.58 to 51.55 FPS (2.77×) while raising VMAF to 98.43 / 99.95.

Beyond tracking, v4.5 cuts 4K VMAF/SSIM/PSNR evaluation from ~50 minutes down to ~4.2 minutes (~12×), upgrades YouTube 4K extraction with Deno EJS challenge solving and CUDA NVENC pre-transcoding (16.8×–23.9× RT), replaces 5 MB Drive chunks with persistent 128 MB streaming PUTs, and ships the cross-device Apple Liquid Glass UI.

Architectural delta snapshot
  • Before: serial FP32 batch=1 tracking + CPU optical flow (37.4 ms/f), full-frame ROI copies, two-pass audio muxing.
  • Now: 5-stage async Batch-8 FP16 + TrackTrack-Fast (~10.7–11.8 ms/f), ROI-slice HFDR (~0.38 ms), 1-pass inline mux.
  • Before: 1-thread libvmaf with 30 FPS upsampling (~50 min at 4K) & 5 MB Drive upload chunks.
  • Now: 16-thread libvmaf overlapped with 128 MB persistent HTTP Drive uploads (~4.2 min at 4K).

Batched GPU Inference + Decoupled Tracker

Replaced single-frame model.track() with greedy Batch-8 FP16 Tensor Core inference (INFER_BATCH_SIZE=8) followed by an independent TrackTrack-Fast worker (gmc_method=none), skipping 16 ms/frame CPU optical-flow stalls while preserving exact TrackTrack thresholds and cutting ID switches up to 57.5%.

ROI-Cropped HFDR, Zero-Copy Pipes & Inline Mux

Eliminated 17.7 ms INTER_AREA reader contention, added explicit (9,9) HFDR blur kernels, cropped apply_roi_hfdr() strictly to cv2.boundingRect(roi_mask) (~27× faster, ~0.38 ms), wrote via zero-copy memoryview(np.ascontiguousarray(out_buf)), and muxed audio inline in one NVENC p4 pass.

128 MB Drive Streaming & 4K CUDA Transcoding

Replaced 5 MB Drive uploads in gh.py with persistent requests.Session 128 MB fixed-Content-Length streaming (_ChunkStreamReader, 256 KiB aligned) plus 32 MB fallback. Upgraded tg.py YouTube 4K ingestion with Deno EJS extraction and zero-copy CUDA NVDEC→NVENC transcoding (CQ 16 p2, 16.8×–23.9× RT).
